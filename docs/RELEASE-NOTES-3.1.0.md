WriteMind 3.1.0

- **A new bar over the note.** One toolbar row that never wraps: Style (it shows the caret's level; its menu lists the text levels, the lists and the cells, with their keys), B I U S, font and colour, List, Quote, Code, the inserts (text box, picture, table, maths, shapes) and Move Section Up / Down. The pen is ONE button with a menu: Pen or Eraser, colour, width, "Pen always draws". What does not fit goes under ⋯.
- **The tab row** holds the sidebar, rendered-page and video buttons, always side by side whether the sidebar is open or not, and a list of every open note.
- **Search notes** (⇧⌘F / Ctrl+Shift+F) over the whole project, titles and text; Enter opens the note with Find on the match. The sidebar's bar is a search field, + (New Note / New Section) and the pencil (Edit); every row has a ⋯ menu and each section's "New note" row takes a dropped note.
- **Undo reaches files.** Ctrl+Z takes back the newest step: your typing, or one of the last three file operations (new note, duplicate, rename, move, trash, new section, section rename / move / trash, Clean Up). Anything a step removed is kept as a backup until the step is more than three back. Edit ▸ Undo names the step.
- **The video pane** has one 36px header: turn left / right, zoom, hold, straighten, Writing | Image | Raw ("Page" is now "Image"), and a toast that says what came into the note. The tablet sheet's Paper, orientation and Bring in are menus; the box's actions put Erase last.
- **The drawing layer:** a picked object has eight resize handles, a rotate handle and one inspector bar (colour, width, fill, label, order, dock into the note, duplicate, delete); Delete fires on click, not on press.
- **Find and Replace** and the link picker float over the note instead of pushing it down; a Replace All is one undo step. Dialogs keep Tab inside them, Move to Trash starts on Cancel, and Keyboard Shortcuts is searchable.
- **Cells and the cursor:** a cell made from the Style menu or the + between cells is one undo step with the caret inside it; Move Section keeps the caret and moves in one step; a list started from a menu takes its first word as written.
- **Pen chords stand down where AltGr types a character**, so { [ ] } @ type on European keyboards.
- **Apple silicon only on the Mac.** One arm64 dmg; no Intel code ships. Intel Macs stay on 3.0.0.
