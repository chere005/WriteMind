WriteMind 3.7.0

- **Rearrange tabs.** Drag a tab sideways along the row, in the notebook or in the video pane (the Live camera tab stays first); the order is kept. Right-click a tab for Move Tab Left / Right, or use Ctrl+Shift+PageUp / PageDown (Window menu).
- **Double-click the divider** between the notes and the video and the video (the camera or the Wacom sheet) takes its full size, the notes keeping their minimum; double-click again to put it back.
- **Wacom sheet's Bring in:** the box's row has Picture (the boxed part as an image); the Bring in menu has Sheet as Picture and Sheet as Text for the whole sheet; "As Drawing Cell" and "As Text" are now Drawing Cell and Text.
- **Text brought in is one cell, and you see it land.** The lines of one reading come in as one cell; bringing text in, or putting the pen down on the notebook, switches the page to the rendered one. A multi-line reading is a text cell as typed, so a mark the reader found (a struck word) shows as its characters.
- **A single newline grows the cell** to include the line the caret is on (paragraph, text and markdown cells), in the source and on the rendered page.
- **The bar keeps half a cell spacing** from the cell it follows: at the top, between cells and at the bottom. The page's top margin is 6px deeper so the + over the first cell is not cut.
- **The + menu works with the mouse.** Pressing a cell type in the + menu now makes the cell and puts the caret inside it, for every kind, on both pages (the menu used to close on the press).
- **A colour picker for imported writing.** Several picked strokes (a Wacom Bring in writing) get colour and width together, and a camera Writing capture (an ink graphic) can be recoloured; one Undo each.
- **Fixed on the Mac:** the arrows of a chart read off a camera picture are routed with right angles on the source page too, and four end-to-end tests that spoke Windows now hold on a Mac.
